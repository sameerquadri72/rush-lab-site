What is Rush Lab?

Rush Lab is a competition show for creators. A group of creators takes on a run of physical, skill-based and unpredictable challenges, and the last one standing wins the cash prize. The first Rush Lab Creators Challenge has 12 creators, 8 challenges and ₹20,000.
